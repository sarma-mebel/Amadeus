The presence update: she has a life of her own, and your desktop is her home.

## She exists alongside you now

- **Her own sleep schedule.** At night she is asleep on her own clock: no
  unprompted chatter, reach-outs wait until morning, and poking her awake
  gets a groggy, mildly indignant answer. Opening the app still works, but
  she greets you as someone you just woke. Hours are configurable
  (`sleep_schedule` in the config).
- **Ambient awareness (desktop).** She notices cheap local signals without
  any screenshots: the same app focused for hours, the battery running low,
  and whatever you are listening to. Every track change is a chance for one
  short line, and she often chooses to say nothing. Window titles and media
  metadata only, never stored, never leaves your machine.
- **Cursor play.** Her eyes already followed your mouse. Now if you park it
  on her and hold still for a moment, she notices and tilts her head at it.
- **Multi-monitor roaming.** Her occasional strolls can cross to your second
  monitor, so the whole desk is her territory rather than one screen.
- **Tuck away.** A new action in the pet's quiet menu slides her mostly off
  the nearest screen edge. She stays quiet out there, leans out when you
  hover the visible sliver, and comes back when you click it.
- **Away-time continuity.** Come back after a few days and she mentions what
  she has been up to (her diary and her own ongoing pursuits), instead of
  acting like time stopped when you left. Reach-outs open the same way a
  friend's text would: one line about her day, then the question about
  yours.

## Getting to know you, and being heard

- **Early acquaintance.** In the first few conversations she is genuinely
  curious about who you are: your name, what fills your days, what you are
  working on. One light question when it fits, never an intake form, and if
  you deflect she drops the subject. The phase ends on its own.
- **A presence dial.** One choice in Settings (Quiet, Balanced, or Lively)
  sets how much unprompted life she has: chatter cadence, thinking sounds,
  listening reactions, desktop roaming. The individual toggles are still
  there underneath.
- **Better pause manners on calls.** With one small optional download
  (`python scripts/fetch_smart_turn.py`, an 8 MB model that runs on CPU),
  full-duplex calls stop cutting you off mid-thought: if your pause sounds
  like the middle of a sentence, she waits.
- **Better hearing.** Voice input now defaults to the whisper "small" model
  with proper beam search instead of "base" with greedy decoding, which
  mis-heard often enough to get frustrating. Configurable via
  `providers.stt_model`.

## Fixes worth knowing about

- Character packs now ship inside the GPU desktop installer, so switching
  characters changes the voice along with everything else (previously an
  installed app had no packs at all, and only the outfit selector worked).
- The pet no longer gets stuck at low render resolution after a busy speech
  burst: going idle restores full resolution immediately.
- The greeting no longer re-asks the question it just asked when you reply
  with a bare "hey".

## Under the hood

- The conversation-quality baseline was re-recorded: 17 scenarios, all
  passing at 7 to 10 out of 10.
- New telemetry around every feature above (`ambient.*`,
  `presence.sleep_suppressed`, `web.duplex_smart_turn_*`), visible in
  Settings, Diagnostics. All zeros is the healthy state, as always.
